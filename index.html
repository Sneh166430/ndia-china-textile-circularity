India–China Textile Waste & Circular Fashion Dashboard

Comparative research on textile waste, recycling systems, sustainable supply chains and circular economy practices.

Researcher: Sneha Yadav

Research Overview

This independent research project examines textile waste management and circular economy initiatives in India and China. It aims to identify evidence-based opportunities for more sustainable apparel supply chains and small businesses.

Country Research Profiles

🇮🇳 India

Focus: Textile waste management, recycling infrastructure, apparel manufacturing and SME sustainability.

🇨🇳 China

Focus: Textile recycling, circular economy policies, manufacturing systems and resource recovery.

Research Questions

  1. How do textile waste management systems differ between India and China?
  2. What recycling and circular economy initiatives are documented in both countries?
  3. What barriers affect sustainable textile supply chains?
  4. What lessons can small apparel businesses learn from these systems?

Research Methodology

Research approach: Comparative secondary research.

Data collection: Government publications, international organization reports and academic studies.

Analysis: Descriptive statistics, data visualization, comparative analysis and evidence synthesis.

Data integrity: Statistics from different years, definitions or measurement systems will not be presented as directly comparable without appropriate justification.

Research Sources

  • Ministry of Textiles, Government of India
  • Government of China
  • United Nations Environment Programme
  • Academic Research Literature

Specific publications, dates and dataset citations will be documented as the evidence database is developed.
